Moving the real OS cursor

No website can move your operating system's pointer — every browser blocks it, because a page that could reposition your cursor could park it over “Allow” and make you click things you never meant to. What this page does instead is take over the whole screen and hide your cursor, so the pointer you see is the one it controls.

If you want the actual OS cursor moved, that has to run outside the browser. One file, no dependencies:

  • mouse_mover.ps1 — Windows. Double-click, or powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File mouse_mover.ps1
  • mouse_mover.py — macOS & Linux. python3 mouse_mover.py

Add --solo / -Solo and it just runs on a timer with no browser at all.
